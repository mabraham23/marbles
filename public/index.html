Quick rules

How to play

1. Goal of the game

Move all of your marbles around the board and into your finish spaces. In team games, your team wins when every teammate's finish spaces are filled.

2. Leaving home

Roll 1 or 6 to leave home. If several marbles are home, only the leftmost one can leave.

Example If marbles 1, 2, and 3 are home, only marble 1 can leave. If 1 and 2 are already out, marble 3 is next.

3. Traversing the board

Normal movement

Most rolls move your marble forward along the connected track holes.

Your own marbles block

You cannot land on or pass through your own marble, so some rolls will not appear as move options.

Corner shortcuts

If your marble is sitting on a corner, a roll can jump forward by that many corners when the path is clear.

Center

An exact roll can move into the center. Later, rolling 1 lets that marble exit to a corner.

4. Taking your turn

4
K1 moves 4 K2 moves 4 K3 enters finish

On your turn, roll the dice. If moves are available, choose one highlighted board spot or one move from the list.

5. When no moves are available

5
Rolled 5: no valid moves

If the roll cannot move any marble, the game shows a short no-move message and then continues.

6. Captures

Landing on another player bumps that marble back home. Your own marbles block your path.

Example If your marble lands exactly on an opponent, their marble returns home and yours takes that spot.

7. Special cases

Rolling 1 or 6 gives you another roll. Finish spaces require an exact roll.

If you leave home with a 1 and immediately roll 3, that marble may move backward toward the gate. This does not apply after leaving with a 6.

Example Leave home with 1, then roll 3: that marble may move backward. Leave home with 6, then roll 3: move normally.
